Separate problems and tickets

Parser: month-first interpretation in a day-first tenant

The shared parser reads dates month first. A day-first tenant’s intended 7 April can become 4 July. This affects app and email intake, plus forms that do not take the booking-v3 hint path. Classify using the tenant’s configured locale and the actual channel, not the reporter’s browser locale.

Parser issue #2 and report membership. Reproduce with an ambiguous date, then verify the intended date survives parsing, storage, invite and reminder. Include a date whose day exceeds twelve to check rejection/validation rather than a silent swap.

Hint: booking-v3 tells day-first users to enter month first

The booking-v3 form prints MM/DD/YYYY while interpreting input according to the day-first tenant locale. The hint encourages an input order that the form then reads differently. The affected form tenants are alder.physio, copper.studio and pale.harbour; their app/email reports still take the parser path.

Hint issue #1 and report membership. Make the displayed hint, validation and interpretation agree. Test each affected tenant on booking-v3, and check booking-v2 plus month-first locale controls. A parser-only change does not establish that the hint is fixed.
